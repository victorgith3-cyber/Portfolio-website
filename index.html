About Me

I'm a passionate web developer aiming to create modern, responsive websites and web applications. I specialize in front-end development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with frameworks like React and Vue.js.

Am also on an exciting journey from IT infrastructure to full-stack development.

Building on a solid foundation as a Certified IT Technician, I possess a deep understanding of systems and problem-solving.

Currently, I specialize in frontend development, crafting responsive and intuitive user interfaces.

My approach combines technical expertise with a keen eye for design, ensuring that every project not only functions flawlessly but also provides an exceptional user experience.

Driven by a love for creating solutions that are both beautiful and functional.

I am now actively expanding my expertise into backend technologies, driven by a passion to bridge the gap between user experience and system architecture as a versatile full-stack developer.

Beyond code, I also design and edit visual content. I use Canva to create promotional posters, social media graphics and marketing materials, and Adobe Premiere Pro to edit video. I am building my camera skills too, with a working knowledge of camera operation that I keep improving through practice.

I also enjoy working with data. At Roots Data, I worked as a Graphics Designer, Data Analyst and IT Technician, where I cleaned and visualized data using Excel. Combining design, data and technical skills helps me build websites and content that look good, communicate clearly and are backed by real information.

When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new technologies, writing poetry, playing Chess, contributing to open-source projects, or hiking in the mountains to clear my mind and find inspiration.
